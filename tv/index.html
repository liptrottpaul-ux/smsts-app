Watch on your TV

Your phone shows the app on the TV over Wi-Fi and stays the remote control. While casting, the sound comes out of the TV. When you stop, it goes back to the phone automatically.

Before you start

  • Phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi.
  • TV switched on (for Fire TV: hold the Home button and choose Mirroring first).
  • Turn Big text for the TV on:

Start casting

  1. Swipe down from the top of the phone screen twice to open Quick Settings.
  2. Tap Cast, Screen cast, Smart View (Samsung) or Screen mirroring – the name depends on your phone. If you can't see it, tap the pencil icon to add it.
  3. Choose your TV, then tap Start now.
  4. Come back to this app. Turn the phone sideways for a bigger picture on the TV.

Stop casting the same way (Quick Settings › Cast › Disconnect). Sound returns to the phone.

If something isn't right

  • Sound still on the phone: open the casting controls (the pull-down notification or the Smart View icon) and choose to play sound on the TV, then turn the phone volume up.
  • TV not listed: check both are on the same Wi-Fi and the TV's screen-mirroring setting is on.
  • Casting stops: keep the app open. The app keeps the screen awake during lessons and exams.
  • Picture lags a little: this is normal with mirroring. Sit closer to the Wi-Fi router.
